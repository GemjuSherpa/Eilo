Reviewed 2026-10-22 against main `23271ca4a8b76045fd43fdf242a749d5d12f6d96`. This is public-document and source review, not device feasibility acceptance. No device settings, permissions, entitlements, accounts or notification schedules were changed.

## VC-SURFACE-03: iOS

The supported candidates are a generic local notification and, only after separate eligibility/design review, a Live Activity. There is no planned arbitrary Siri-style overlay. [Apple User Notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/) does not guarantee delivery. [Authorization guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/asking-permission-to-use-notifications) requires checking current authorization before scheduling. Permission can change; denial must leave Eilo usable in the foreground. Focus, notification settings and system presentation control delivery/timing. Eilo must not promise a 20-second banner or wake/launch itself to show results.

A future local fallback must contain only fixed operational copy (“Results ready”, explicit “Open Eilo”), with no response/query/source/media/identifier in title, body, userInfo, attachment, category action or URL. Tapping may open the app, but must not resume capture/speech or recover old content without current native session eligibility. Notification delivery can outlive the process; generic content must remain safe after lock, termination and revocation. Removing pending/delivered notifications is cleanup, not a guarantee that the OS never presented them. Permission and current lock-state checks alone cannot prevent a lock-after-schedule race.

[Live Activities](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities) need a WidgetKit extension, runtime availability/authorization and lifecycle/error handling. They have bounded lifetimes and no direct network/location access in their sandbox. They do not establish background microphone or inference eligibility. [Apple design guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities) calls for innocuous summaries of sensitive content and tasks with a defined beginning/end. Eilo has not established a suitable activity, so Live Activities are deferred, not an approved route. [App Review 2.5.4](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) restricts background services to intended uses; silent audio/VoIP or other mechanisms must not be used to maintain an overlay or bypass lifecycle restrictions. No store approval is inferred.

Source audit: the shipping iOS target has no notification-result adapter or ActivityKit/widget target. S14 clears content on background/lock; S08 remains HOLD. VC-AUDIO-10 and physical authorization/terminated-app/lock journeys remain open. VC-SURFACE-03 is PARTIAL_DOCUMENT_REVIEW_ONLY; VC-SURFACE-04 implementation is dependency-blocked.

## VC-SURFACE-01: Android

[Settings.canDrawOverlays](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/provider/Settings) and [SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/Manifest.permission) require manifest declaration and explicit special-access approval. Eilo currently declares neither that permission nor an overlay service. POST_NOTIFICATIONS and the existing microphone foreground service are separate capabilities; neither authorizes an overlay. A future UI must start off, explain and request its own consent in the foreground, recheck the system grant, and remove all result content on revoke, lock, Stop, expiry or stale session. Refusal must keep the foreground app usable without automatically opening it.

[TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/view/WindowManager.LayoutParams) is below critical system windows; the system can adjust its visibility/position. Other apps can [hide overlays on Android 12+](https://developer.android.com/security/fraud-prevention/activities). Permission does not promise continuous visibility or OEM lifecycle survival. No accessibility-service or full-screen-intent workaround is proposed. A bounded movable card must preserve underlying touch/Back/gesture navigation, expose native Stop and close, and clear on uncertain lock state; those properties need physical tests.

[Microphone foreground-service requirements](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/14/changes/fgs-types-required) include independent permissions and while-in-use restrictions. Overlay consent must never start microphone capture, enable background mode or claim an exemption from those restrictions. [Play permission review](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9214102) is assessed during release; this source review is not Play approval or a completed permission declaration. Actual target policy, prominent disclosure and store submission require review of the final implementation.

VC-AUDIO-09 and physical Android overlay permission/lifecycle/OEM evidence are unavailable. VC-SURFACE-01 is PARTIAL_DOCUMENT_REVIEW_ONLY; VC-SURFACE-02 is dependency-blocked. No physical Android device or supported OEM list is assumed.
